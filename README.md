# 🍫 Unwrapping Global Chocolate Sales Insights with Choco Sales Spark Dashboard  

> 🌍 *A Data Analytics Project organized by **Edu-Linking**, guided by **Prof. Dr. Rezaul Karim**, and certified by **Brit College of Engineering & Technology (BCET)** — transforming global chocolate data into sweet, actionable insights.*

---

## 🚀 Project Overview  
This **Power BI dashboard** visualizes **$3.5 million in global chocolate sales** across multiple countries, using **interactive maps, KPI cards, and product analytics** to uncover sales dynamics, product performance, and shipping efficiency.  

🍬 **Peanut Butter Cubes** emerge as the star product (~$525 K in sales), while **Organic Choco Syrup** lags, revealing key opportunities for market repositioning.  

📍 Major markets like **the USA**, **India**, and **the UK** dominate revenue share — helping stakeholders pinpoint where to scale marketing, inventory, and logistics strategies.  

---

## 📊 Key Insights Unwrapped  

### 🇺🇸 1. USA — The Chocolate Powerhouse  
- The **USA** drives **over 30% of total revenue**, contributing heavily to the $3.5 M total.  
- 🧭 *Action Point:* Prioritize marketing and loyalty programs in the U.S. to sustain top-tier growth.  

---

### 🇮🇳 2. India — High Volume, Low Margin  
- Around **80 K boxes shipped globally**, but **India ships more boxes per sales dollar** than the USA.  
- 🚚 *Action Point:* Optimize shipping routes and costs in high-volume regions for better margin efficiency.  

---

### 🍫 3. Peanut Butter Cubes — The Bestseller  
- Generates **~15% of global sales (~$525 K)**, driven by demand in **Australia** and **the USA**.  
- 📦 *Action Point:* Expand stock allocation and distribution for this high-performing product line.  

---

### 🌿 4. Organic Choco Syrup — The Underperformer  
- Represents **<10% of sales (~$350 K)** despite steady international demand.  
- 🧃 *Action Point:* Launch targeted promotions or bundling with premium products to improve visibility.  

---

### 🇬🇧 5. The UK — High Value, Low Volume  
- Generates **~$700 K** in sales but ships fewer boxes than India — implying **higher-value product positioning**.  
- 💷 *Action Point:* Assess **pricing strategy** and **SKU diversity** to expand shipment volumes sustainably.  

---

### 🇧🇷 6. Emerging Markets — Untapped Sweet Spots  
- **Brazil** and other smaller markets contribute **<5% of total revenue (~$175 K)**.  
- 🌎 *Action Point:* Deploy localized marketing to diversify global revenue and reduce dependency on top markets.
