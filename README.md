# 🍫 Unwrapping Global Chocolate Sales Insights with Choco Sales Spark Dashboard  

> 🌍 *A Data Analytics Project powered by **Power BI**, designed to unwrap hidden trends behind $3.5M in global chocolate sales — revealing how flavor, geography, and logistics drive sweet success.*  

---

## 🚀 Project Overview  
This **Power BI dashboard** visualizes **$3.5M in global chocolate sales** across multiple countries, combining **interactive maps, KPI cards, and product breakdowns** to help businesses optimize inventory, marketing, and logistics.  

🍬 Key spotlight: **Peanut Butter Cubes** lead global demand (~$525K), while **Organic Choco Syrup** trails behind, offering opportunities for targeted promotions.  

📍 Major markets such as **the USA**, **India**, and **the UK** emerge as performance anchors, guiding smarter strategic decisions for international growth.  

---

## 📊 Key Insights Unwrapped  

### 🇺🇸 1. USA — The Chocolate Powerhouse  
- **Over 30% of total revenue** comes from the **USA**, contributing heavily to the $3.5M total.  
- 🧭 *Action Point:* Strengthen marketing efforts and promotional campaigns in this core market to sustain growth momentum.  

---

### 🇮🇳 2. India — High Volume, Low Margin  
- Around **80K boxes shipped globally**, but **India ships more boxes per sales dollar** than the USA.  
- 🚚 *Action Point:* Streamline **logistics and shipping efficiency** in high-revenue zones like the USA to cut operational costs.  

---

### 🍫 3. Peanut Butter Cubes — The Bestseller  
- Contributes **~15% of global sales (~$525K)**, driven by **Australia** and **USA** demand.  
- 📦 *Action Point:* Increase stock allocation and regional availability to capitalize on consistent demand peaks.  

---

### 🌿 4. Organic Choco Syrup — The Underperformer  
- Accounts for **<10% of sales (~$350K)** despite stable demand.  
- 🧃 *Action Point:* Introduce **bundled offers** or **cross-category promotions** to lift visibility and market penetration.  

---

### 🇬🇧 5. The UK — High Value, Low Volume  
- Generates **~$700K** in sales yet ships fewer boxes than India — signaling **higher product value** or **premium pricing**.  
- 💷 *Action Point:* Analyze **pricing strategies and product mix** to identify scalable sales opportunities without sacrificing margin.  

---

### 🇧🇷 6. Emerging Markets — Untapped Sweet Spots  
- Regions like **Brazil** contribute **<5% of total sales (~$175K)**.  
- 🌎 *Action Point:* Explore **localized marketing campaigns** to diversify revenue sources and reduce dependence on top-performing countries.
